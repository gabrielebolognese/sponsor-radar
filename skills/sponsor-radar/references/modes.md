# Modes

The mode comes from subscriber count (`channel.py: mode_for`, `bands_for`). It changes who gets scanned, how the rate is priced, and how you frame results and pitches.

| Mode | Size | Core band (subs) | Bigger band | Rate | Framing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **UGC** | 0-1k | 2k-50k, a fixed floor | 50k-300k | per asset ($100-300 to start) | Brands that pay for content, plus affiliate programs. Pitch the portfolio, not the reach. |
| **Micro** | 1k-50k | 0.5x-3x you (top end at least 5k) | up to 10x (at least 50k) | median views x CPM | Brands sponsoring your lookalikes. Offer hybrid deals (flat fee + affiliate). |
| **Conquest** | 50k+ | 0.3x-3x you | up to 20x | median views x CPM | Brands that sponsor competitors but never you, renewals, agency contacts, rate checks. |

**"Your size" in ranking** compares median views, not subscribers. A creator counts as "your size" when their median views are 0.5x-3x yours. UGC mode uses the subscriber band instead, because a sub-1k channel's views are too noisy to compare.

## What to emphasize when presenting

### UGC

- The lookalike sample is 60% core band, 20% small creators (500-1,999 subs) and 20% bigger. The small slice is there to find the growth target: the smallest creator a brand actually paid.
- Most sub-1k channels get `REALISTIC=no`. In that case, lead with the growth target and `growth-plan.md` (see SKILL.md step 9), not with the brand list.
- The headline is the brands that pay small creators.
- Then the "Affiliate programs your lookalikes use" section from the report. Those are the easiest first yes.
- The rate is per asset: brands buy the video, not the views.
- Tell the user to build a 3-video portfolio page before pitching, if they don't have one yet.

### Micro

- Lead with the headline numbers and the top 3 by score.
- Point out brands with a repeat label. Those are the ones to pitch first.
- If a brand has both `sponsored` and `code` evidence, mention that a hybrid deal is realistic.

### Conquest

- Lead with the "never sponsored you" count and the renewal list.
- If the user gave their current rate, show the rate check prominently.
- Flag brands that only sponsor bigger creators as stretch targets.
